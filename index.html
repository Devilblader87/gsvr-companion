GSVR Companion

The free PC app for the GSVR worlds in VRChat: play ROSE Online, Ragnarok Online, Counter-Strike 1.6, the Half-Life campaign, Sven Co-op and Warcraft III — built from your own installed games, on your PC. About 5 minutes to set up.

Worlds: GSVR - ROSE Online (MMORPG in co-op; help and setup on Discord), GSVR - RO (Ragnarok Online, alpha; bring your own game: needs your own Ragnarok Online install; help on Discord), GSVR - Half-Life (the campaign in co-op, Opposing Force, Blue Shift, Sven Co-op and more; needs Half-Life on Steam) and GSVR - Counter-Strike (classic rounds, bomb, hostages, bots; needs Counter-Strike on Steam). Each world only needs the game it is built from.

Already installed? The companion updates itself on start — nothing to download again. Portable zip and source: see all releases.
Windows or your browser warns you? That is expected.
  • Browser (Chrome / Edge: “not commonly downloaded”): open the downloads list, then … → Keep (Edge: Keep anyway).
  • Windows: “Windows protected your PC” / “unknown publisher”: click More info → Run anyway.
Why: GSVR is a free fan project, and code-signing certificates cost money every year, so the installer is not signed. Instead, everything is open: the source code is public under the GPL-3.0 (GitHub), and every release page lists the SHA-256 of each file so you can check your download. No account, no login, and the app sends no data about you: it only talks to VRChat on your own PC (127.0.0.1) and to GitHub for its updates.

Get playing in 4 steps

  1. Download GSVR-Setup.exe (button above). For Half-Life / Counter-Strike you also need the game on Steam, installed on this PC; for ROSE Online, help and setup are on Discord; for Ragnarok Online you need your own Ragnarok Online client installed on this PC. The companion finds Steam by itself.
  2. Run GSVR-Setup.exe — no admin rights needed, it starts with Windows and updates itself.
    Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” (new, unsigned app): click More info → Run anyway.
  3. In VRChat: Settings → Comfort & Safety → Allow Untrusted URLs: on (or type “untrusted” in the settings search field).
  4. Start GSVR Companion (Start menu or its tray icon) and rejoin the world. The START HERE board at the spawn turns green when everything is ready.
Ragnarok Online (alpha, bring your own game)
  1. Install your own Ragnarok Online client and start it once so it finishes updating.
  2. Install and start GSVR Companion. It looks for Ragnarok Online by itself; if it is not found, choose the game folder (the one with data.grf) in the Ragnarok Online row.
  3. In VRChat: Settings → Comfort & Safety → Allow Untrusted URLs: on.
  4. Open the world GSVR - RO (or press “Open world” in the companion). The first visit builds the maps, characters, monsters, interface and sounds from your own install on your PC; this takes a few minutes and is cached.
  5. New in 0.9.63: the original Ragnarok windows, ROSE-style laser/click controls, monsters on all maps (culvert, dungeons, MVPs), the first jobs, skills on monsters, and sound and music from your install.
  6.   7. New in 0.9.64: the Prontera lobby with the original character select, a smooth VR laser with hover highlight, the original skill and status windows, a controls window with rebinding, second jobs, cards and pets, more maps, Kafra storage, NPC shops, status effects, heal and buffs on party members, elemental arrows, 16 players fighting together, all MVPs, and a Fly/Butterfly Wing fix.
Nothing from the game is in the world. Help on Discord.

Why a companion app?

The world does not contain any game files. The companion reads maps, models and sounds from the copy of the game that you own, converts them on your PC and hands them to VRChat over 127.0.0.1 (your own computer only). Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and only games you have installed can be played.

Trouble?

  • "Companion not found" — the app is not running, or a firewall blocks local connections.
  • "Untrusted URLs" — switch the VRChat setting above on and rejoin.
  • "Game not installed" — install it in Steam, the companion rescans automatically.
  • VRChat on PC (Windows) only — Quest / Android cannot load local files.
